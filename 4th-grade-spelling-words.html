How to practice these words

  • Say it, spell it, say it again — hearing the word before and after spelling cements it.
  • Short daily sessions beat cramming — 10 minutes a day outperforms an hour before the test.
  • Mix up the practice — write them, type them, spell them out loud, find them in puzzles.
  • Focus on the misses — re-test only the words your child got wrong last time.
